# Fantasy Balls Constitution

**Fantasy Balls** · Est. 2022 · Commissioner: **Will Golder**

Football ESPN ID: **225969362** · Baseball ESPN ID: **1126511192**

This document is the league's rule book. ESPN runs the platform; these rules govern how *we* run the league. If something is not written here, it is not a house rule.

## 1. Identity

- League name: **Fantasy Balls**
- Sports: Football and Baseball
- Established: **2022**
- Commissioner: **Will Golder**

## 2. Dues

- Dues are **$25** per manager, per season, for each sport that manager plays.
- Dues must be **paid in full before that season's draft**.
- A manager who has not paid in full before the draft **forfeits their first-round pick** in that draft. The pick is not retained, traded, or deferred unless the commissioner states otherwise in writing to the league before the draft starts.
- Failure to pay does not remove other obligations the manager already owes under league business.

## 3. Keepers

Applies to **both** Football and Baseball.

1. Each manager may keep **a maximum of three (3)** players. A manager may keep fewer than three, including zero.
2. A player drafted in the **1st or 2nd round** of the previous draft **cannot** be kept, even if that player was later traded or picked up on waivers.
3. Keeping a player costs the draft pick in the **same round** that player was selected in the previous draft.
4. If the same player is kept in **consecutive** years, the cost moves **one round higher** each consecutive year (example: 6th, then 5th, then 4th).
5. A player who was not drafted and was acquired as a **free agent** costs a **10th-round** pick. Additional free-agent keepers cost the next higher rounds in order: **10th, then 9th, then 8th**, and so on. Consecutive-year free-agent keeps follow the same one-round-higher progression.
6. Players acquired by **trade** may be kept, subject to every rule in this section. Cost is based on that player's prior draft round or free-agent status under rules 3–5.
7. A player is only eligible to be kept if they were on a fantasy roster for **meaningful playing time in the prior season**. Players added only in the offseason, or only at the end of the season for the purpose of keeping them, are **not** eligible. The commissioner decides eligibility under this rule when it is disputed. That decision is final for that season.

Keeper lists must be submitted by the deadline the commissioner sets before the draft. A player not properly submitted by that deadline is not kept.

## 4. Football

- Platform: ESPN Fantasy Football, league ID **225969362**.
- Format: **Head-to-head points**.
- Keepers: **Section 3** only.
- Number of teams, roster slots, bench, IR, scoring values, waiver rules, trade deadline, regular-season length, playoff size, and playoff seeding are the settings locked in ESPN for that season **before the draft**, unless the league votes to change them under Section 8.
- Managers must set lineups before each period's ESPN lock. Empty slots and missed locks stand as played.
- Draft order and draft type are as announced by the commissioner before the draft. Unpaid managers are subject to Section 2.

## 5. Baseball

- Platform: ESPN Fantasy Baseball, league ID **1126511192**.
- Format: **Head-to-head points**.
- Keepers: **Section 3** only.
- Number of teams, roster slots, scoring values, waiver rules, trade deadline, regular-season length, playoff size, and playoff seeding are the settings locked in ESPN for that season **before the draft**, unless the league votes to change them under Section 8.
- Managers must set lineups before each period's ESPN lock. Empty slots and missed locks stand as played.
- Draft order and draft type are as announced by the commissioner before the draft. Unpaid managers are subject to Section 2.

## 6. Trades

- Trades process through ESPN under the league's trade settings for that season.
- A trade is vetoed if **five (5)** league members vote to veto within the veto window ESPN provides.
- The **commissioner may veto any trade**. A commissioner veto does not require five member votes.
- Vote totals and commissioner vetoes are final once the window closes or the veto is issued.

## 7. Tanking

- Managers must play to win in the current season.
- Intentionally losing games, benching starters without a competitive reason, or making moves whose main purpose is to improve a future draft position or future roster at the expense of the current season is **tanking**.
- If the commissioner determines tanking occurred, the commissioner may reverse transactions, adjust lineup results for the affected period, or apply draft penalties for the following season. The commissioner's determination for that incident is final.

## 8. Changing the rules

- A change to this constitution requires a **majority vote** of league members.
- A vote is valid only if the proposed change is stated clearly to the league before voting.
- Rules in effect at the start of a draft remain in effect for that season unless a majority vote says otherwise and the commissioner announces the change before it applies.

## 9. Enforcement

- The commissioner administers these rules and the league settings in ESPN.
- Disputes about what a rule means are decided by the commissioner, except where this document already gives a fixed number or deadline.
- "Majority" means more than half of the managers in that sport's league for that season.

## 10. Maintaining this document

- The commissioner keeps the published constitution updated to match votes that have passed.
- Only the published version on the league site is official.

## 11. Weekly chug

- Each scoring period, the manager with the **lowest points scored** in that period owes the league a **chug**.
- The chug must be **recorded on video and posted in the league chat** before the next period's lineup lock.
- If the required video is not posted by that deadline, that manager's team is **penalized 15 points** in the **following** scoring period.
- The commissioner tracks compliance. Ties for lowest score: all tied managers owe a chug unless the commissioner announces a different tie-break before the period starts.
- This rule applies to **Football and Baseball**.
